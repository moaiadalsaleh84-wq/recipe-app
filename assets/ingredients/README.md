# Ingredient photos

Pantry Pop shows a real photo next to ingredients (recipe page, cooking checklist, pantry).
Each ingredient is matched to one of the kinds below; one photo per kind covers every ingredient of
that kind. Until a kind has a photo, the app shows no picture for it (never a drawing or emoji).

## How the photos should look (so they match each other)

- Real food photography, **natural daylight**, soft shadows, realistic texture.
- The ingredient alone, **centered, filling about 70%** of the frame.
- The **same plain background** for every photo (a warm white or light stone surface).
- Square, **512 × 512 px**, JPEG, under 80 KB each.
- Licensed for use in an app sold on the App Store (for example your own photos, a paid stock pack,
  or a licence that allows commercial use without attribution in the app).

## Adding a photo

1. Save it here as the file name in the table, e.g. `tomato.jpg`.
2. Add one line to `src/components/ingredientPhotos.ts`:
   `tomato: require('../../assets/ingredients/tomato.jpg'),`
