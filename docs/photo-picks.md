# Photo picks

One photo per recipe, chosen by the owner. Nothing here is picked yet.

**Only** Unsplash or Pexels (free for commercial use). Never TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Google Images or recipe blogs.

Each photo must: show exactly this dish · close-up or 45° angle · bright natural light · appetizing · sharp · no text, watermarks or faces · at least 1600 px wide.

> Candidates couldn't be listed yet: searching needs the official Unsplash and Pexels APIs, which need free API keys from the owner. Until then, each recipe has search links. Paste up to 3 candidate links per recipe below (or ask Claude to fill them once the keys are added), then mark the one you pick with ✅.

After a pick: crop 4:3 (cards) and 1:1 (thumbnails) centred on the food, WebP under 300 KB, blurhash placeholder with fade-in, and an entry in src/data/photoCredits.ts (URL, photographer, license).
